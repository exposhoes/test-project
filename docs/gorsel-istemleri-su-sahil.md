## water.png — nehir ve deniz suyu
Seamless tileable top-down texture of clear blue river water for a blocky voxel game, soft turquoise and medium blue tones, gentle small ripples and a few subtle white light glints, stylized but realistic, no objects, no text, evenly lit, square 512x512, edges tile perfectly.
